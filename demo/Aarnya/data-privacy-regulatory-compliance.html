01 / Services

Aaranya Consulting helps businesses address data privacy and regulatory compliance requirements through legal research, documentation support and compliance assessments.

Privacy policies

Consent & data practices

Compliance plans

02 / Legal framework

Understand applicable obligations

Our regulatory compliance services include legal research, compliance gap assessments and the preparation of compliance checklists and action plans. We help businesses understand their obligations under applicable data protection and sector-specific regulations, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, taking into account applicable provisions and implementation timelines.

03 / Data protection

Review practices and response procedures

We assist with drafting and reviewing data protection documentation, including privacy policies, data processing agreements, contractual data protection clauses and internal procedures for handling personal data. Our support also covers reviewing consent mechanisms, data collection and retention practices, and procedures for responding to data principal requests, as applicable under the relevant legal framework.

04 / Risk & recommendations

Strengthen privacy and compliance practices

We also assist with reviewing data breach response procedures, internal reporting protocols and contractual obligations relating to data protection. Through structured legal documentation, regulatory analysis and practical recommendations, Aaranya Consulting helps businesses identify compliance risks and strengthen their approach to data privacy and regulatory requirements.
