01 / Services

Support across litigation support and eDiscovery services

Aaranya Consulting provides litigation support services to assist legal teams and law firms with case preparation, document review, legal research and evidence organisation.

Document review

Review and categorise case-related documents to support efficient case assessment.

Case files & indexes

Organise case files, prepare document indexes and make records easier to navigate.

eDiscovery & ESI

Structure, categorise and review electronically stored information and digital documents.

Factual summaries

Prepare factual summaries, document chronologies and case timelines.

Legal research

Review legislation, judicial precedents and legal issues; prepare concise case-law summaries and research notes.

Deposition summaries

Summarise testimony to help legal teams navigate relevant facts efficiently.

02 / eDiscovery

Organise digital information

Our eDiscovery support focuses on the organisation and review of electronically stored information (ESI) and digital documents. We help legal teams structure, categorise and review relevant records systematically, making information easier to access and assess during litigation preparation.

03 / Research & timelines

Make case information usable

We also assist with preparing factual summaries, document chronologies, case timelines and structured research notes. Our legal research services include reviewing relevant legislation, judicial precedents and legal issues, supported by concise case-law summaries and research notes for counsel and in-house legal teams.

04 / Case preparation

Support focused litigation preparation

In addition, we prepare deposition summaries to help legal teams navigate testimony and identify relevant facts efficiently. Through structured document management, organised case records and focused review support, we help streamline litigation preparation and enable legal professionals to work with case-related information more effectively.
